# DEF-002 — Cart total ignores product quantity

## Classification
Deliberately introduced training defect in QA Demo Shop.

## Status
Reproduced; fix awaiting verification.

## Severity
High within this practice application: the incorrect total also feeds
the simulated checkout amount. No real payment processing exists.

## Environment
Local Node.js server, Playwright, Chromium.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the store.
2. Add Wireless Mouse, priced at ₹499.
3. Change its quantity to 2.
4. Leave the quantity field.
5. Observe the cart total.

## Expected result
Cart total: ₹998.00.

## Actual result
Quantity becomes 2, but cart total remains ₹499.00.

## Automated evidence
Command:
npx playwright test cart.spec.js --project=chromium

Observed result:
CART-001 failed: expected 998.00, received 499.00.

Saved report:
evidence/DEF-002/failing-report/index.html

## Root cause
The total calculation adds each product's unit price without
multiplying it by the selected quantity.

## Proposed fix
Restore price multiplied by quantity in the total calculation.
